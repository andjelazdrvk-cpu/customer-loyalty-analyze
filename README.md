# Customer Loyalty Analyzer

A simple Python application for analyzing customer purchase history, determining loyalty status and calculating discounts.

## Features

- Collects the customer's name and purchase history
- Calculates total spending
- Counts purchases above 10,000 RSD
- Determines whether the customer has VIP or STANDARD status
- Applies a discount to a new purchase based on the customer's status

## Loyalty Rules

- **VIP:** total spending above 100,000 RSD and more than 10 purchases
- **STANDARD:** customers who do not meet the VIP requirements
- **VIP discount:** 10%
- **STANDARD discount:** 5%
